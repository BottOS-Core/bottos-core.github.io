Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Bott is a desktop AI assistant for Windows. This policy explains what data Bott uses, where it goes, and the choices you have. We wrote it in plain language on purpose.

The short version

  • Your chats, files, memory, tasks and account live on your own PC. Bott does not upload them to a Bott server.
  • To answer you, Bott sends your message to an AI service (for example Google Gemini or Groq) through Bott Cloud. Only what's needed for that answer is sent.
  • We never sell your data and never show ads.
  • You can export, back up or delete your data at any time in Settings → Privacy & data.

What stays on your PC

  • Your Bott account (email and a securely scrambled password — we can't read it)
  • Chats, agents, projects, tasks, calendar, reminders, memory and settings
  • Files you add to Bott, and files Bott makes for you (in Documents\Bott)
  • Daily backups (Documents\Bott\Backups) and your usage counts for your plan
  • Sign-in tokens for apps you connect (Google, Spotify, etc.)

What leaves your PC, and why

What Sent to Why
Your message, recent chat context and attachments you send AI providers: Google Gemini, Groq, NVIDIA, GitHub Models, OpenRouter, Hugging Face, Cloudflare Workers AI, and (only if enabled) Anthropic or OpenAI To write the answer
Web searches Bott runs for you Search providers (e.g. Tavily, DuckDuckGo) To find current information
Your requests to AI services Bott Cloud (our Cloudflare Worker) It adds Bott's service keys and passes the request on. It does not save your messages. It keeps short-lived request counts per internet address for fair use.
Payment details you enter: plan, amount, UPI transaction ID (UTR), Bott ID, optional email Bott Cloud (stored) To check and approve your payment
Problem reports you choose to send: your description, optional email, Bott version, and (if you tick the box) recent technical logs with keys, emails and phone numbers removed Bott Cloud (kept up to 60 days) To fix bugs
Actions you ask for in connected apps (send an email, add an event…) That app (e.g. Google) To do what you asked

AI providers handle your messages under their own privacy policies. Don't share passwords, bank details, OTPs or other very sensitive information in chats.

Google account data (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Contacts)

When you sign in with Google, Bott also connects your Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive and Google Contacts (you see this on Google's page before you allow it). Bott uses this data only to show it to you inside Bott and to do what you ask, for example reading or sending an email, adding a calendar event, finding a file or calling a contact. - The sign-in tokens for Google are stored on your PC, not on a Bott server. - Parts of an email, event or file are sent to an AI provider only when you ask Bott to work with them (for example "summarise my last email"). - Bott does not sell this data, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train AI models. People at Bott do not read it. - You can disconnect Google anytime in Integrations, or remove Bott's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Bott's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your PC and screen

Bott can open apps, read files, look at your screen and run commands only when you ask it to do a task. Risky actions (deleting, sending to people, payments, shutting down) ask for your approval first. Bott does not watch your screen in the background.

Voice

"Hey Bott" wake-word listening runs on your PC. Your voice is turned into text either on your PC or by a speech service, and then handled like a typed message.

Children

Bott is meant for people aged 13 and above. If you are under 18, please use Bott with your parent or guardian's permission, and they should read this policy too. If you're a parent and want your child's data deleted, email us.

Your rights (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023)

You can: - See and export your data: Settings → Privacy & data → Export - Correct it: edit or delete chats, memory and settings anytime - Delete it: Settings → Privacy & data → Delete all local data, then uninstall Bott. For data in Bott Cloud (payment records, problem reports), email us and we'll delete what we're not required to keep. - Withdraw consent by stopping use of Bott and uninstalling it.

Security

Passwords are stored scrambled (hashed). Bott's service keys stay in Bott Cloud, never in the app. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your PC protected and up to date.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and show what changed in the app.

Contact & grievances

Email bott.oscore@gmail.com for any privacy question, request or complaint. We aim to reply within 7 days.
