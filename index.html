Questions

Is Bott free?

Yes. The Free plan has no time limit and needs no card. Bott Plus adds higher limits for people who use it all day.

Which computers does it run on?

Windows 10 and Windows 11 (64-bit). Mac and phone versions aren't available yet.

Does Bott read my email?

Only when you ask it to, for example “summarise my unread emails”. Your Google sign-in is stored on your PC, and you can disconnect Google anytime in Integrations or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Which languages does it speak?

Bott understands and replies in English, Hindi and 30+ other languages, by text and by voice. The app itself can be switched to 35 languages.

Can it control my PC?

Only when you give it a task: opening apps, finding files, changing settings and similar. Anything risky, like deleting files, sending messages or shutting down, asks you first.

What do I need for phone calls?

Microsoft Phone Link, which is free from the Microsoft Store, and your phone linked to it. Bott shows the setup steps on its Calls page until your phone is connected.

How do I uninstall?

From Windows Settings, then Apps. Bott asks whether to keep your data, so a reinstall can pick up where you left off.
